## 0.6.0 - 2026-08-30

- Added location anchors, character blocking, reflection topology, giant-scale anchors, impossible-space rules, and a reverse spatial pass to reduce teleportation, wrong exits, duplication, and scale drift.
- Added long-source and series facts, character knowledge, timeline, reveal state, source slicing, and resume cursors for serialized continuity and large-text adaptation.
- Added request fingerprints, idempotency keys, job state, cancellation/recovery, concurrency leases, and candidate lineage; unknown jobs must be recovered before any resubmission.
- Added MUST-HIT-first blind candidate review, single-variable retries, and a separate reviewer role so attractive but unusable takes do not win by default.
- Added a formal edit timeline, usable ranges, media normalization, stems, editable delivery, and pre-release technical gates.
- Added five production templates, five validator families, and regression coverage while keeping named case studies and peer products out of Skill instructions.

- Removed named case-study and open-source-project attribution while retaining executable general production rules.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-08-26

- Added comedy-legibility and animated-performance guidance: 60–120 second work now establishes a macro causal spine and planted major reversal before local gags, and each gag must change macro state.
- Required every featured gag to record normal state, visible cause, audience advantage, character misread, reaction hold, reveal, and consequence; invalid eyeline or occlusion fails acceptance.
- Added a photoreal-world/real-fur plus animated-actor performance mode with pose/expression masters, anticipation, overshoot, settle, and controlled deformation while still rejecting penetration, teleportation, count drift, and content mutation.
- Added background reaction characters, event-load budgets, and per-line speaker/mouth binding; a cat mouthing a staff line is now P0.
- Expanded character, prop, block, shot, audio, and QA templates, with validator checks for comedy contracts and active-speaker fields.

## 0.3.3 - 2026-08-26

- Added a recurring/cross-scene/text prop bible: lock multi-view geometry, scale, material, and state with a validated high-fidelity image route, then carry `prop_id/state_id/text_plate_id` through storyboard, generation, and acceptance.
- Separated exact text from prop geometry and defaulted to one tracked transparent PNG/vector plate in post, preventing stamps, scales, and packages from changing shape or copy across scenes.
- Compiled reversing, steering, detachment, and collision into start state, control input, world/screen vectors, phase sequence, and stable outcome; reject any pixel motion that contradicts the plan.
- Added an impact-shot structure and Hero Insert route for motivated near-lens occlusion, scale growth, camera response, synchronized sound, and visible consequence at hooks/collisions/climaxes.
- Expanded continuity, block, shot, and QA templates; the validator rejects contact plans without a physical-event contract or impact shots without an impact pattern.

## 0.3.2 - 2026-08-25

- Added an optional high-density 60–120s Douyin pacing preset: conflict by 3 seconds, an immediate objective by 10 seconds, and 18–24 effective beats per 90 seconds.
- Added 15–25-second micro-task loops plus multiple dialogue turns and 4–7 visible actions/reactions per roughly 30-second voiced scene.
- Required one macro goal across the film and a causal bridge for every scene change, while stating that effective beats do not equal cuts or generation requests.
- Added a Wan 3 reference-driven multi-character native-dialogue route with one-to-one image/video/voice binding, full-block performance, clean native audio, R2V/frame-lock exclusivity, and input-video duration budgeting.
- Integrated the preset into both Skill languages, story/directing references, and production briefs, with a new evaluation scenario and automated regression test.
