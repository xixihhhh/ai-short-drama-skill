# Example: Paw Emergency Service — Unit Episode

Recurring cats run a secret night service that solves tiny household crises using only feline abilities. Every episode is self-contained.

## Episode contract

- Cold open: a visually impossible household problem in the first three seconds.
- Orange cat acts first and makes it worse through confidence.
- Blue-eyed cat studies vibration, smell, airflow, or sound and finds the rule.
- Both solve it with cat-feasible tools: weight pedals, bite loops, rolling objects, paw-sized controls.
- Final fee is always a ridiculous cat desire; the feline instinct ruins the victory pose.

## Sample episode: The Refrigerator Moon

A round night light rolls behind a refrigerator. The cats believe the moon has fallen into the kitchen. Their rescue escalates from paw fishing to a counterweighted ribbon, until the refrigerator door starts opening. They must save the light without releasing the forbidden tuna smell. The reveal: the human had put the light there to guide an elderly parent at night. The cats restore it, then charge one tuna flake—before both faint theatrically at the smell.

The emotional reveal is planted by an earlier slow human step and cane shadow; it does not introduce an unrelated story.
